# Research and import conventions

Target: 14 categories × 3 tiers × 3 references = 126 distinct exact models. Categories: pc-fixes, portables, processeurs, cartes-graphiques, cartes-meres, ram, ssd, boitiers, alimentations, refroidissement, ecrans, claviers, souris, casques.

Research current manufacturer pages and market availability; record date, exact reference, source URL and confidence. A recommendation is an editorial choice, not a verified sales ranking. Consider performance/value, use, compatibility, transport weight and warranty geography. Do not infer reliability statistics from brand reputation.

Keep a structured master with explicit unknowns. Export a global CSV and a WooCommerce draft-only CSV. Use stable SKUs, UTF-8, properly quoted fields, category-specific normalized units and manufacturer part numbers when documented. Prices observed during research are separate from EDoctor sale prices. Never infer EDoctor stock from a supplier listing.

A product row must distinguish sourced specs, missing specs, current availability and image/reuse status. Images belong to `catalog/images/<category>/<sku>/`; provenance links go in the master. Use exact official/authorized images with true alpha, never generated reconstructions. Pending rights or missing photographs must remain visible in the completeness report. Do not create fake PNGs or silent substitute photographs.

Validate row counts, unique exact models/SKUs, tier distribution, paths, alpha, numeric units and draft import. Import into real WordPress is a separate authorized action. Global WooCommerce attribute taxonomies must be created/mapped before import.
